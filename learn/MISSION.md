# Mission: Understand and present the ethikit workflow

## Why
Present ethikit as a project demo and be able to explain, from the inside, what each step of
its workflow does and why -- including answering "isn't this just ranking models?" and
"is a model's ethicality even inherent?" without notes. (DRAFT -- confirm with the user.)

## Success looks like
- Walk an audience through data -> models -> tools by principle -> profile -> audit trail, naming what each step does.
- Explain why ethikit is selection-and-audit rather than ranking, and where its claims stop (e.g. the meta-benchmark is not per-domain).
- Open the console's inspect tab and explain any number's formula and what the intermediate table shows.

## Constraints
- Short lessons; learner is presenting soon (timing to confirm).

## Out of scope
- Writing new evaluators; deep fairness-metric mathematics (until the workflow itself is solid).
